## Personal rules (from the scaffold)

- **Language**: reply in the language I write in. Files (code, comments, docs, commit messages) stay in the
  project's working language, English unless the project says otherwise.
- **Projects made with the scaffold** have their rules in `AGENTS.md` and their routines in `docs/process/`; follow
  them. To scaffold or update a project, use the `scaffold` skill; to propose a generic improvement to the scaffold
  from any project, the `scaffold-propose` skill.
